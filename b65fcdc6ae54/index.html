04 Engine 1

B2B supply: no stock without a purchase order

The partner's industrial customers send an RFQ. We find the goods, quote, and ship only against a PO with a deposit. No marketplace fees, no ads.

RFQ comes inFrom a buyer the partner can reach. We ask for a redacted RFQ or PO first.
We source and quoteIndia, or China for some lines. Nobody has been contacted yet, so prices are public listings or ESTIMATES.
PO plus 30 to 50% depositThe model uses 40%. No deposit, no order.
Supplier gets 30% at order, 70% before shipmentOne consolidated sea LCL shipment of about 9.4 cbm carries all lines.
9 weeks from PO to deliveryProduce, sea LCL, clear, truck.

Rules: credit cap of one month of sales per buyer. Bad debt provision 1.5%. One late payment ends repeat credit.

Every AED 1 of monthly sales ties up about AED 0.7 of working capital before profit. At 40% deposit, 9 weeks to deliver, 30 days credit.

    07 Risks

    One condition decides Engine 2

    ZENVEX is a Sharjah free zone company. Amazon fulfilment centres are on the mainland. We need one written answer from Shams and Sharjah Customs. Try both answers.

    May ZENVEX import in its own name and deliver to a mainland FC?Cost of the answer: one phone call. Alternative if no: a mainland distributor invoice.

    08 The plan

    20 weeks, with gates

    Week 1 is the week of 16 Nov 2026. Engine 1 runs first. Amazon starts only after the written free zone answer and three quotes. Nothing is bought or contacted until the owner says send.

    Owner (India)Partner (UAE)AI teamJointDecision gate
    Owner (India)Quotes and samples from 2 suppliers per SKU, production, packing, export papers, freight booking.
    Partner (UAE)Licence amendment, written answers, customs code, clearing agent, trucking, B2B pitches.
    AI teamFee previews, listings, cost model updates, ad plans, weekly report, quote comparison.

    Stock is buyable around week 11 (late January 2027), before Ramadan (expected 8 Feb 2027). The Amazon test slips if the free zone answer is late. For gifting, POs and deposits must be signed by about 10 Dec 2026 or skip it.
